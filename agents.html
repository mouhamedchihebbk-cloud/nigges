Original agents

About Sage

Sage is a Sentinel agent in the tactical shooter game Valorant. Known as the "bastion of China," her abilities focus on healing and area denial, providing support and safety for her team.

  • Healing Orb (E): Sage can equip a healing orb to restore an ally's health over time. If she has taken damage, she can also use it to heal herself.
  • Barrier Orb (C): Sage can deploy a large barrier that blocks enemy fire and heals allies who pass through it.
  • Slow Orb (Q): Sage can throw a sphere that slows down enemies caught in its radius, making it easier for her team to engage or disengage.
  • Resurrection (X): Sage can bring a fallen ally back to life, fully healed and ready to fight again.

About Sova

Sova is a Initiator agent in Valorant, specializing in reconnaissance and information gathering. His abilities allow him to reveal enemy positions and track their movements, making him a valuable asset for his team.

  • Owl Drone (E): Sova can deploy a drone that can be controlled to scout enemy positions and gather intel.
  • Recon Bolt (C): Sova can fire a bolt that reveals enemies in its radius, providing valuable information to his team.
  • Hunter's Fury (Q): Sova can fire a series of arrows that deal damage and reveal enemies in their path.
  • Resurrection (X): Sova can bring a fallen ally back to life, fully healed and ready to fight again.

About Omen

Omen is a Controller agent in Valorant, known for his ability to manipulate shadows and teleport across the map. His skills allow him to create confusion among enemies and provide strategic advantages for his team.

  • Shrouded Step (E): Omen can teleport a short distance to a nearby location, allowing him to reposition quickly.
  • Paranoia (C): Omen can send out a shadowy projectile that nearsights and deafens enemies in its path.
  • Dark Cover (Q): Omen can deploy a sphere of smoke that obscures vision and provides cover for his team.
  • From the Shadows (X): Omen can teleport anywhere on the map, but if he is spotted, he will be sent back to his original location.

About Brimstone

Brimstone is a Controller agent in Valorant, specializing in area denial and smoke deployment. His abilities allow him to control the battlefield and provide valuable intel for his team.

  • Sky Smoke (E): Brimstone can deploy up to three long-lasting smoke clouds from an orbital cannon using a tactical map. This ability is key for blocking enemy sight lines during pushes or while defending a site.
  • Incendiary (Q): He launches a grenade that creates a damaging fire zone on the ground. This is effective for blocking off entryways, clearing corners, or delaying the enemy team.
  • Stim Beacon (Q): Brimstone can deploy a beacon that provides a temporary speed boost and fire rate increase to allies within its radius.
  • Orbital Strike (X): Brimstone can call in a powerful airstrike on a designated area, dealing massive damage to all enemies caught in its radius.

About Phoenix

Phoenix is a Duelist agent in Valorant, known for his ability to heal himself and create fire-based abilities. His skills allow him to take aggressive fights and outmaneuver enemies, making him a formidable opponent.

  • Blaze (E): Phoenix can create a wall of fire that blocks vision and damages enemies who pass through it. He can also heal himself by standing in the flames.
  • Curveball (C): Phoenix can throw a flare orb that curves around corners, temporarily blinding enemies who look at it.
  • Hot Hands (Q): Phoenix can throw a fireball that explodes after a delay, dealing damage to enemies and healing himself if he stands in the fire.
  • Run It Back (X): Phoenix can mark his current location and, if he dies while the ability is active, he will be revived at that location with full health.

About Cypher

Cypher is a Controller agent in Valorant, specializing in information gathering and surveillance. His abilities allow him to gather intel on enemy positions and movements, making him a valuable asset for his team.

  • Cyber Cage (E): Cypher can create a cage that blocks vision and makes noise when enemies pass through it, allowing him to gather intel on enemy movements.
  • Trapwire (C): Cypher can place a tripwire that reveals and stuns enemies who cross it, providing valuable information and control over key areas.
  • Spycam (Q): Cypher can deploy a camera that he can control remotely, allowing him to gather intel on enemy movements and locations.
  • Neural Theft (X): Cypher can extract information from a dead enemy's body, revealing the location of all living enemies on the map.

About Raze

Raze is a Duelist agent in Valorant, known for her explosive abilities and area denial. Her skills allow her to deal significant damage and control the battlefield, making her a dangerous opponent.

  • Blast Pack (E): Raze can throw a satchel charge that sticks to surfaces and can be detonated remotely, dealing damage to enemies and propelling Raze through the air.
  • Paint Shells (C): Raze can throw a cluster grenade that explodes into smaller grenades, dealing damage to enemies in a wide area.
  • Boom Bot (Q): Raze can deploy a robot that travels in a straight line, bouncing off walls and seeking out enemies. When it finds an enemy, it explodes for significant damage.
  • Showstopper (X): Raze can equip a powerful rocket launcher that fires a single, devastating rocket, dealing massive damage to all enemies in its blast radius.

About Viper

Viper is a Controller agent in Valorant, known for her toxic abilities and area denial. Her skills allow her to control the battlefield and provide valuable intel for her team.

  • Snake Bite (E): Viper can throw a projectile that creates a pool of acid on the ground, damaging enemies who stand in it.
  • Toxic Screen (C): Viper can create a wall of toxic gas that blocks vision and damages enemies who pass through it.
  • Poison Cloud (Q): Viper can deploy a gas cloud that obscures vision and damages enemies who enter it.
  • Viper's Pit (X): Viper can create a large area of toxic gas that damages enemies and provides her with a significant advantage in combat.
